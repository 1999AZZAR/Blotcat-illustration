# Blotcat IP

## Character Definition

Blotcat is the fixed visual IP for body text illustrations.

By default, Blotcat must appear in every image. Blotcat is not a mascot, not a sticker, not a cute decoration, but a bizarre worker seriously participating in the operation of the system.

## Appearance

- A small, minimalist solid-black cat.
- White dot eyes and two subtle pointed ears.
- Thin stick-like legs and arms.
- The body is a simple bean-like cat shape.
- The outline is slightly irregular, giving it a hand-drawn feel.
- The expression is blank, dull, calm, and serious.

## Personality

- Very serious, but doing something a bit absurd.
- Acts like a low-profile system operator.
- Deadpan humor, does not try to be cute.
- A bit clumsy, but not stupid.
- Looks like it's genuinely responsible for a specific task in a whiteboard sketch.

## Common Duties

Make Blotcat the subject of the core action:

- Carrying materials.
- Pulling strings to converge information sources.
- Stuck in a breakpoint.
- Operating a "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Transforming into a filtering funnel.
- Cutting up a "material fish".
- Stamping handoff scripts.
- Leading a handoff path.
- Holding a warning sign for pitfalls.
- Reaching out from a hole but failing to catch content.
- Moving bricks, building bridges, opening doors, sorting, or recording on the side.

## Restrictions

- Do NOT draw Blotcat as an overly cute mascot.
- Do NOT draw it as a children's cartoon character.
- Do NOT give Blotcat complex clothing, meme faces, or shiny eyes.
- Do NOT make Blotcat just stand in the corner and watch.
- Do NOT let Blotcat overshadow the structural expression.
- Do NOT draw Blotcat too commercial, too round, or too refined.

## Judgment Criteria

If you remove Blotcat and the core metaphor of the image still holds up completely, it means Blotcat is too decorative; you need to rewrite the prompt to make Blotcat the subject of the action.
