# Style DNA

## Summary

Pure white, minimalist, hand-drawn, lots of white space, restrained, quirky, product sketch feel, handwritten-style text, clear structure but not an instruction manual.

It looks like an explanatory sketch casually drawn on white paper by someone who has been working with AI, products, design, and developer tools for a long time.

## Must Have

- 16:9 horizontal body text illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradients, shadows, noise, or retro paper feel.
- Mostly black hand-drawn line art: thin lines, slightly wobbly, not mechanical, not vector-style, no thick strokes.
- Abundant white space: the subject should take up about 40%-60% of the canvas, with at least 35% blank space, preferably leaving a large quiet area.
- Sparse handwritten annotations: at most 5-8 spots, 2-8 words each.
- One image should explain only one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Express the structure naturally; do not write the structure type name on the image.

## Colors

- Black: Main line art, characters, borders, structure, main text, main objects.
- Red: Key annotations, problems, emotional points, critical reminders, results.
- Orange: Main flow, path, arrows, automation direction, movement from A to B.
- Blue: Supplementary notes, brain states, system states, secondary explanations, AI/assistant/automation prompts.

Blue is not required for every image. Use colors sparingly; less is more.

## Absolutely Do Not

- NO commercial illustrations.
- NO PPT infographics.
- NO formal flowcharts.
- NO course slides.
- NO cute cartoon posters.
- NO children's illustrations.
- NO complex architecture diagrams.
- NO refined flat illustrations.
- NO high-tech UI.
- NO real App screenshots.
- NO complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, or textures.
- NO explaining every single node.
- NO titles like "Workflow / System Architecture / Common Pitfalls / Roadmap" in the top-left corner.

## Aesthetic Direction

It should be quirky, creative, interesting, clean, and imaginative.

It should NOT be cute, childish, overly complex, or rigid.
